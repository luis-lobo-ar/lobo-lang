Bytes index/count units are raw bytes; Text index/count units are Unicode scalars. slice arguments are start,count, not start,end. at returns I64. Buffers are immutable; concat/push return new values. Text::bytes gives UTF-8 bytes; no implicit Text/Bytes conversion. Byte bounds, byte values outside0..255, invalid slice ranges and invalid UTF-8 in from_bytes fault at runtime, not by static type rejection. Text::valid reports UTF-8 validity as Bool.
